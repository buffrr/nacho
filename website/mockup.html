No QR on mobile. You're already on the device with the wallet — a QR to scan with the phone you're holding is the one thing nobody needs. Render it only above the breakpoint.

Everything else stacks in the same order: identity, pay, records. That's the layout you already have, so mobile needs no work.

Truncate harder. At 330px a Lightning offer gets 8 characters, which is enough to confirm it's there but not to read. The row is a tap target, not a display — full value belongs on tap.

The breakpoint is 1180px, not 900. Below that the sidebar and the record values are both squeezed, and two cramped columns are worse than one comfortable one.

Worth deciding

Verify is a claim you can't back on the web. The button is in the sidebar because it's the honest version of that green tick — it should explain that the site got these from a relay, and offer the app as the way to check for yourself. If it just shows a tooltip saying "verified", it's worse than not having it.

"updated 3d ago" needs a source. If the relay gives you a publish timestamp, good. If you're inferring it, drop it — a wrong freshness claim on a page about verifiable records is a bad trade for a small detail.

The sidebar can hold more later — public key, certificate state, namespace link. It has room now and it's the natural home for facts about the handle rather than things the handle points at.
